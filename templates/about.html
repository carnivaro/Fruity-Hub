About Fruity Hub

Connecting Jos with fresher choices.

A simple web-based marketplace designed around the fruit trade at Jos North Fruit Market.

Our purpose

Making fresh fruit shopping more convenient.

Fruity Hub connects customers with fruit vendors in Jos North Fruit Market through an easy-to-use online platform. Customers can browse available products, view details, add items to a cart and submit delivery information without needing to visit the market physically.

The platform also gives vendors a practical way to have their products represented online while keeping the shopping process simple for customers.
